## Yoann Frayce

AI engineer and founder. I build systems that decide under uncertainty —
simulation, agents, multimodal and local AI.

The same line keeps showing up across five codebases and three languages: a
language model may read, explain and narrate, but it must not produce the number
someone acts on. The arithmetic belongs in deterministic code, and the boundary
belongs in tests rather than in prompt wording.

### Current product work

**FleetMind** — simulation-driven decision systems for EV fleet operations.
Private development; technical case study available on my [portfolio][portfolio].
